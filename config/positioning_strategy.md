# Positioning Strategy

**Goal**: consistent content signal that makes Himanshu discoverable and
credible for engineering-leadership / management-track roles (Director of
Platform Engineering, Head of Infrastructure, VP Engineering, or similar),
building toward his C-suite/board-level trajectory.

## Why this matters for reach (not just vanity)

LinkedIn's 2026 ranking does semantic matching, not keyword matching -- it
reads the *meaning* of your recent posts and profile together to decide whose
feed to surface you in. That means scattered, unrelated daily-news reactions
actively work against being recognized as an expert in a specific area.
Staying inside 2-3 consistent pillars is what builds that signal over weeks.

## Content pillars (rotate across these, don't chase every news item)

1. **Platform engineering & infra strategy** -- EKS/Cilium/AWS decisions
   through a business-impact lens (cost, reliability, org velocity) --
   not pure how-to content. This is the credibility anchor.
2. **Technology + business bridge** -- applying EMBA frameworks (Akerlof,
   Casadesus-Masanell & Ricart, diffusion of innovation, etc.) to real
   tech-industry events. This is the differentiator most infra people don't
   have -- lean into it.
3. **Sustainability / circular economy in tech** -- e-waste, EPR compliance,
   PunarChakra-adjacent themes. Smaller pillar, but ties your venture work
   into your professional brand coherently.

## Natural keyword presence (never force these -- they should read like they
belong, not like SEO stuffing)

engineering leadership, platform strategy, cross-functional decision-making,
infrastructure ROI, technology-business alignment, org-level velocity,
build-vs-buy, technical debt as a business decision, scaling engineering teams

## What the Writer Agent should NOT do

- No engagement-bait closers ("Agree? Comment below!", "Like if you've faced
  this too") -- these get actively suppressed in 2026 ranking.
- No external links inside the post body -- reduces reach. If a source link
  is needed, it goes in a suggested first-comment, generated separately.
- No keyword stuffing -- one or two natural mentions per piece, not a list.

## Separate, one-time actions (not automatable by this pipeline)

These move the needle on recruiter visibility more than any single post, and
they're a you-task, not a pipeline-task:
- LinkedIn headline: make sure it names the management-track terms you want
  to be found for, not just current title.
- About section: 2-3 lines bridging platform engineering + EMBA + strategic
  leadership intent.
- Turning on "Open to Work" (private mode, recruiters-only) if/when you want
  that signal active.
